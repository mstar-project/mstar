# Lockstep mechanisms on main (fa381afd6)

Paths are relative to the checkout root. Invariant numbers refer to AGENTS.md. Invariant 11 states the lockstep rule and invariant 7 the async worker's stage order, and the blocks below point to them instead of restating them.

### 1. Lockstep instance and leader role
- **Does:** Defines a node's TP×SP block as one lockstep instance and makes instance rank 0 its only scheduler.
- **Where:** `JointGroups` at mstar/distributed/communication.py:264 (`rank` at :269, `world_size` at :277), and `WorkerParallelGroups.get_instance_rank_for_node` and `get_instance_world_size_for_node` at :467 and :470. The worker derives `parallel_leader_nodes`, `parallel_nodes` and `is_tp_follower` at mstar/worker/worker.py:554, :564 and :575. The engine derives `_tp_leader_nodes` and `_tp_follower_nodes` in `Engine._classify_tp_roles` at mstar/engine/engine.py:2506.
- **Keeps:** Exactly one rank per instance, rank 0 in both its TP and SP groups, initiates work on a parallel node, and a worker hosts at most one lockstep node (worker.py:568). Every other block assumes one leader whose decisions the rest replay.
- **Used by:** `MicroScheduler._scan_ready` at mstar/worker/micro_scheduler.py:697 and `pending_rows` at :1306, `Worker._is_tp_follower_node` at worker.py:2454, `Engine.offload_request` and `reload_request` at engine.py:2452 and :2471, and the paged KV manager at mstar/engine/resources/kv/manager.py:571.
- **Extend via:** Ask `get_instance_rank_for_node(node) == 0` or `Worker._is_tp_follower_node(node)`. The tempting duplicate is a leader test on `get_tp_config_for_node(node).rank == 0`, which elects one leader per TP row of a tp×sp instance (comment at worker.py:546-553).
- **Breaks if:** A change mixes the two meanings of "TP size". On `WorkerGraph` and the conductor's sharding groups, `tp_size` and `_tp_rank` are the instance size and instance rank (rewritten at mstar/model/base.py:236 and stamped at mstar/conductor/conductor.py:713), which is why worker.py:986 and :1058 are correct for SP too. The TP `CommGroup` carries per-axis values.

### 2. Process-group creation schedule
- **Does:** Creates every TP and SP subgroup on every rank in one globally sorted order before serving.
- **Where:** `GlobalParallelConfig` at communication.py:556 builds `world_parallel_groups`, the sorted union of TP and SP rank tuples, at :603. `WorkerParallelGroups.init_dist` at :368 creates them in the loop at :416, NCCL on every rank and gloo twins members-only at :426-429.
- **Keeps:** Every rank makes the same device `dist.new_group` calls in the same order, so `group_count` names them alike and no member waits on a non-member. The gloo twins are members-only and torch names them from `len(_world.pg_names)`, which the device groups advance only on their members, so on a 2-D mesh (`cosmos3_nano_tp2_sp2`) the members of a twin hash different names and main hangs at startup (#705). The gloo rule is the last paragraph of invariant 11.
- **Used by:** `Worker.__init__` at worker.py:452 and the conductor at mstar/conductor/conductor.py:716. Every `CommGroup.device_group` and `cpu_group` comes from here (communication.py:441-442).
- **Extend via:** A new group adds its rank tuples to `world_parallel_groups` in `GlobalParallelConfig`, so `init_dist` creates it with the rest. The tempting duplicate is a lazy `dist.new_group` inside a resource, model or runner the first time it is needed.
- **Breaks if:** A group is created on a path only some ranks reach, or in an order that follows per-rank dict iteration, or a members-only group's members reach it having registered different numbers of groups.

### 3. Setup-time backend votes
- **Does:** Has every rank of a comm group vote on whether it could build an optional collective backend, and keeps the backend only if all could.
- **Where:** `_all_ranks_agree` at mstar/distributed/all_reduce.py:37 (gloo MIN over `cpu_group`), used by `AllReduce.new` at :53 with the vote at :87. `_all_ranks` at mstar/distributed/ar_fusion.py:28 is an identical copy, used by `create_workspace` at :34 with the vote at :62.
- **Keeps:** All ranks of a group run the same all-reduce kernel family, so no rank waits in a symmetric-memory or FlashInfer kernel while a peer is in NCCL. `create_workspace` tries backends in one fixed order (`_BACKENDS`, ar_fusion.py:25), and every rank falls back together when the vote itself fails (:63-71).
- **Used by:** `CommGroup.init_all_reduce` at communication.py:78 from `init_dist` at :447, `CommGroup.init_allreduce_fusion` at :135 from mstar/model/qwen3_5/qwen3_5_model.py:887, and `FlashInferAllReduce.__init__` at all_reduce.py:235.
- **Extend via:** A new backend joins `SYMM_BACKENDS` (all_reduce.py:255) and goes through `AllReduce.new`, or votes with `_all_ranks_agree`. Two copies of the helper already exist, and a third private copy in a new file is the duplicate to push back on.
- **Breaks if:** A per-call kernel choice follows a per-rank property. `MultimemAllReduce._sum_into` (all_reduce.py:209) chooses by message size, which all ranks share, and handles a misaligned output locally with a copy for this reason (comment at :211-213).

### 4. Capture agreement and per-spec barriers
- **Does:** Reduces every capture decision across the joint group and barriers once per spec, so all ranks keep, drop, order and size the same accelerator graphs.
- **Where:** `agree_across_ranks` at mstar/engine/accelerator_graph_runner.py:99 (device `all_gather` over TP then SP, with `torch.amin` to AND flags and `torch.amax` for the max). `AcceleratorGraphRunner` (:653) uses it in `size_lent_captures` at :886, `warmup_and_capture` at :933, :940 and :973, and `_buckets_captured_everywhere` at :1016. `PiecewiseAcceleratorGraphRunner` (:1639) uses it at :1835, :1895, :1899 and :1937. The barriers are at :850-851, :954-955, :1797-1799 and :1914-1916.
- **Keeps:** Invariant 11 names this mechanism. A bucket is registered only if every rank captured all its slots, and costs and capture order are the max over ranks, so every rank walks the same specs in the same order.
- **Used by:** Capture sizing and capture in `Engine` at mstar/engine/engine.py:689, :698, :767 and :775.
- **Extend via:** A new capture-time decision passes its per-bucket flags or costs through `agree_across_ranks`, in an order derived from config, and keeps one barrier per spec. Its tests are test/modular/test_accelerator_graph_capture.py and test/modular/test_capture_budget.py. The tempting duplicate is a second all_gather helper, or a vote over `tp_group` alone that misses the SP axis.
- **Breaks if:** A capture loop skips its barrier with `continue` or `break` on a per-rank failure, or the candidate order comes from per-rank state such as measured free memory instead of the config.
- **See also:** `worker-scheduling.md` › Capture groups, `engine-resources.md` › Capture configs and bucket selection.

### 5. Startup fences and symmetry checks
- **Does:** Brackets warmup with global barriers and refuses to serve when per-rank settings the protocol depends on disagree.
- **Where:** `WorkerParallelGroups.barrier_all` at communication.py:526, called at worker.py:4347 and :4362. `Worker._verify_tp_async_sched_agrees` at worker.py:2469, called at :4348. `KVCacheManager.post_warmup_validate` at kv/manager.py:3418, run from engine.py:728-729. `restore_default_timeout` at communication.py:542, called at worker.py:4363.
- **Keeps:** No leader sends a `ScheduleTPNode` to a follower still inside warmup, every rank of an instance has the same `MSTAR_TP_ASYNC_SCHED` view, and every paged KV pool starts with the same free-page count on every rank. After warmup a wedged rank aborts its peers on torch's default timeout instead of the long load timeout.
- **Used by:** `Worker.run`. The docstring of `_handle_allocation_failure` (worker.py:2336-2338) relies on the KV check.
- **Extend via:** A new per-rank knob that changes what the leader sends or a follower expects gets an all_gather-and-compare check shaped like `_verify_tp_async_sched_agrees`, run between the two `barrier_all` calls. Allocator symmetry for a new resource goes in its `Resource.post_warmup_validate` (mstar/engine/resources/base.py:417). The tempting duplicate is a per-step assertion on the scheduling path.
- **Breaks if:** A check raises on one rank before a collective its peers enter. engine.py:728 runs `post_warmup_validate` for each resource in turn and only the paged KV one is collective, so a local raise in an earlier resource on one rank leaves its peers in the KV `all_gather` until the warmup timeout.

### 6. ScheduleTPNode broadcast
- **Does:** Sends each step the leader runs on a parallel node to every follower as one `ScheduleTPNode`, stamped with a monotonic seq and carrying the leader-only decisions the step depends on.
- **Where:** `Worker.maybe_send_zmq_to_tp_followers` at worker.py:1723, with the counter `_tp_broadcast_seq` at :583. `ScheduleTPNode` at mstar/utils/ipc_format.py:185 carries `request_ids` as wire strings (:188), `spec_seq` and `spec_from_seq` (:190-191), `resident_delta` (:192), `walks` and `walk_idx` for combined walks (:195-196), `prefix_hits` (:198) and `page_trails` (:199).
- **Keeps:** Every leader step reaches every follower once, in send order on a per-peer FIFO (worker.py:3162), with rows in the leader's batch order. The journal drain and the lease decision run once per step, and each follower gets its own copy of the delta (comment at :1734-1736).
- **Used by:** The three submit paths: the speculative head at worker.py:4280, the yield-away batch at :4314 and the non-speculative fallthrough at :4840. Followers receive it at :1286-1287.
- **Extend via:** New per-step replicated state is a typed field on `ScheduleTPNode`, filled inside `maybe_send_zmq_to_tp_followers` and applied in `settle_tp_follow_delta` (block 8). Its tests are test/modular/test_tp_spec_wire_format.py. The tempting duplicate is a new `WorkerMessageType` per decision, which carries no seq and is applied outside the step's FIFO position.
- **Breaks if:** A new path submits a leader step on a parallel node without calling this, calls it after the submit, or puts a worker-local handle in a field (invariant 14). A field type the wire codec cannot encode falls back to pickle once per follower per step (invariant 6).
- **See also:** `worker-scheduling.md` › TP lockstep follow, 7. Follower FIFO and serial follow path, 8. Follower settle seam, 14. TP async scheduling protocol.

### 7. Follower FIFO and serial follow path
- **Does:** Queues the leader's steps on each follower and runs them in order, all rows or none, ahead of the follower's own work.
- **Where:** `MicroScheduler` at micro_scheduler.py:206 owns `tp_batches_pending_schedule` (:260) and `pending_tp_follow_count` (:287). The entry points are `register_tp_follow` at :364, `peek_tp_follow` at :376, `pop_tp_follow_head` at :427, `pop_ready_rids` at :442 and `_try_schedule_tp_follow` at :508, which `get_next_batch` tries first at :590. `Worker._register_tp_follow` at worker.py:3088 is the arrival gate.
- **Keeps:** A follower never starts work on a parallel node by itself (`_scan_ready` at :697), and a popped head is always submitted whatever this rank's local failure state, because the leader is already committed to its collective (comment at :582-589). `pop_tp_follow_head` is the only exit (:428-430), so `pending_tp_follow_count` and `last_consumed_tp_seq` are settled in one place.
- **Used by:** The non-speculative schedule at worker.py:4293, :4817 and :4822, `_try_follow_speculation` at :2926 and :3062, `_await_tp_follow_step` at :3143-3148, `_close_tp_follow_step` at :3112-3114, and the drain gate at :1095.
- **Extend via:** A new consumer of leader steps peeks with `peek_tp_follow`, settles with `settle_tp_follow_delta` and pops with `pop_tp_follow_head`. A follower's reaction to a refused step mirrors the leader's verdict instead of acting on its own, as `_handle_unservable` (worker.py:2304) and `_handle_allocation_failure` (:2365) do. Its tests are test/modular/test_tp_follow_targeted_gate.py. The tempting duplicate is a second queue or a direct `popleft()`.
- **Breaks if:** A follower filters a head's rows by local state (`failed_rids`, `held_until`, a readiness shortcut), drops a popped head, or lets a targeted `get_next_batch` serve the FIFO. The speculation merge may reject what it is handed, so micro_scheduler.py:590 skips the FIFO whenever `target` is set. A rid the follower cannot resolve maps to `REMOVED_RID` and holds the whole head (:463-476), so a head that can never be satisfied stalls after one warning.
- **See also:** `worker-scheduling.md` › TP lockstep follow, 6. ScheduleTPNode broadcast, 8. Follower settle seam, 14. TP async scheduling protocol.

### 8. Follower settle seam
- **Does:** Applies everything a head carries for the follower's resource state before the follower builds that step.
- **Where:** `MicroScheduler.settle_tp_follow_delta` at micro_scheduler.py:400. It replays the delta through `_apply_delta_from_message` (:394), then checks page trails and adopts prefix leases through the engine (:416-425).
- **Keeps:** Neither the serial nor the async follow path builds a step against half-replayed state. It returns False until every move lands, and turns a divergence into `SystemExit("TP ranks diverged")` (:422-425) instead of failing requests, which would leave the peers in the collective.
- **Used by:** `_try_schedule_tp_follow` at micro_scheduler.py:523 with `caught_up=True`, and `Worker._try_follow_speculation` at worker.py:2948 with `caught_up` taken from N's `commit_done`.
- **Extend via:** The follower-side apply of a new `ScheduleTPNode` field goes here, resumable across polls and idempotent per seq (the `_last_resident_delta` guard at :395 is the pattern). The tempting duplicates are applying it on arrival in `_register_tp_follow`, before earlier steps have admitted, or in only one of the two consumers. The docstring at :403-407 records that the async path once skipped the delta and polled forever.
- **Breaks if:** The apply cannot resume. It runs on every poll until it returns True, so partial progress has to be remembered, as `OffloadDelta.pop_left` does.
- **See also:** `worker-scheduling.md` › TP lockstep follow, 6. ScheduleTPNode broadcast, 7. Follower FIFO and serial follow path, 14. TP async scheduling protocol.

### 9. Resident-set journal
- **Does:** Records every offload and reload the leader executes on a parallel node, in order, ships them on the next `ScheduleTPNode`, and has followers replay them verbatim instead of choosing victims.
- **Where:** `OffloadDelta` at ipc_format.py:115. On the engine, `_resident_delta` at engine.py:475 is filled by `offload_request` (:2445) and `reload_request` (:2468), drained by `take_resident_delta` (:2529) and replayed by `apply_resident_delta` (:2542). The follower keeps owed moves in `MicroScheduler._pending_resident_deltas` (micro_scheduler.py:290) through `_apply_resident_delta` (:381).
- **Keeps:** A follower's resident set equals the leader's at the point each step was broadcast. Followers refuse to offload or reload on their own (engine.py:2452-2456 and :2471-2475), and a partial leader reload is undone, so every journaled move is one a rank in the same state can apply (:2487-2490 and :2534-2536). The ordered-not-summarized requirement is invariant 11.
- **Used by:** The leader's eviction in `Worker._try_offload_cold_request` at worker.py:1526, the follower's early return in `_handle_allocation_failure` at :2365, the broadcast at :1738, and the follower's `pop_ready_rids` with `allow_reload=False` at micro_scheduler.py:478.
- **Extend via:** A new kind of page move goes through `Engine.offload_request` or `reload_request` so it is journaled, or adds an action to `OffloadDelta` as another parallel list. Its tests are test/modular/test_resident_delta.py and test/modular/test_kv_offload.py. The tempting duplicates are a resident-set field holding a set of rids, or an LRU pick on the follower.
- **Breaks if:** A leader path calls a resource's `offload` or `reload` directly and bypasses the journal, or a page move lands between a step's broadcast and its admit (block 12). `prefix_cache_tp` with `cpu_offload_pages > 0` is refused at startup because reloads are not ordered against cache hits (kv/manager.py:497-503).
- **See also:** `worker-scheduling.md` › Admit refusal and OOM eviction, `engine-resources.md` › Eviction, offload and the TP resident journal.

### 10. Prefix-cache lease replication
- **Does:** Has the leader lease each row's cached prefix pages as it sends the step and ship the page ids in `prefix_hits`, so followers adopt exactly those pages.
- **Where:** `Engine.decide_cached_prefix` at engine.py:1102, `adopt_cached_prefix` at :1125, `release_cached_prefix` at :1132 and `drop_cached_prefix` at :1138. The resource hooks `lease_cached_prefix`, `adopt_cached_prefix` and `release_cached_prefix` are at mstar/engine/resources/base.py:163-177, implemented by the paged KV manager at kv/manager.py:800, :821 and :857.
- **Keeps:** Every rank's lease names the same page ids, so a sharded prefill gathers the same cache slots. Adoption waits while this rank's commit of the writing step lags (`caught_up` False) and raises once nothing more can arrive (kv/manager.py:824-852).
- **Used by:** `maybe_send_zmq_to_tp_followers` at worker.py:1739, `settle_tp_follow_delta` at micro_scheduler.py:421, `_settle_speculation` at worker.py:2644, `_close_tp_follow_step` at :3116, `_plans_ahead` at :2444, and the voided-head branch of `_await_tp_follow_step` at :3146. Startup gates are at kv/manager.py:496-510.
- **Extend via:** A new caching resource implements the three hooks and leaves the replication to the engine. Its tests are test/modular/test_kv_prefix_tp.py. The tempting duplicate is a follower running its own prefix-index lookup, which can see a different index state at that moment.
- **Breaks if:** A step is built and then dropped on one rank without releasing its lease, or a leader path takes pages outside rank 0's order. `_plans_ahead` exists for the second case: a pre-plan refused on rank 0 alone puts its pages back at the end of rank 0's free list (worker.py:2445-2447).
- **See also:** `engine-resources.md` › Prefix cache, 11. Page-trail divergence check.

### 11. Page-trail divergence check
- **Does:** Under `MSTAR_KV_DEBUG_ASSERTS=1` with `prefix_cache_tp`, hashes each paged KV arena's allocation sequence so a follower compares it with the leader's before every step.
- **Where:** `PageArena.trail` at kv/manager.py:82, advanced in `acquire` at :93-94 and switched on at :574-575. `Resource.page_trail` at base.py:179, `StepRunner.page_trails` at mstar/engine/resources/runner.py:165 (resources with a trail are selected at :102-105), and `Engine.page_trails` and `check_page_trails` at engine.py:1153 and :1156.
- **Keeps:** A page-id divergence is reported at the first step after it happens, with node, walk and seq. It is a debug check and off by default (`_DEBUG_ASSERTS` at kv/manager.py:66).
- **Used by:** `maybe_send_zmq_to_tp_followers` at worker.py:1740 and `settle_tp_follow_delta` at micro_scheduler.py:420.
- **Extend via:** A new allocator returns `(count, hash)` from `page_trail()` and the runner picks it up. The tempting duplicate is an ad hoc all_gather of free-page counts during serving, which is itself a collective on the scheduling path.
- **Breaks if:** Someone treats it as a production guard. With the flag off `page_trails` is empty and nothing is compared.
- **See also:** `engine-resources.md` › Prefix cache, 10. Prefix-cache lease replication.

### 12. Admit fence
- **Does:** Blocks the worker's main thread at the top of every loop iteration until the in-flight step has admitted.
- **Where:** `Worker._await_admit_settled` at worker.py:2217, called at :4531, waiting on `ExecutingBatch.launch_started_event` (engine.py:356). The engine releases the event just before the forward launch (engine.py:1649-1652), and the GPU thread's `finally` releases it on every exit (worker.py:2190-2191). `_ADMIT_FENCE_TIMEOUT_S` at :103 fires only if the GPU thread died.
- **Keeps:** No main-thread page move (deferred removes, drains, delta replay, `check_ready` reloads) lands between a step's broadcast and its admit, which is the timing half of invariant 11. It also makes `admit_refused` knowable when `_build_speculation` runs (worker.py:4234-4246).
- **Used by:** The loop body after :4531, starting with `_apply_pending_removes_safe_to_drop` at :4533, `_apply_pending_drains` at :4540, `_process_messages` at :4548 and `_apply_removes_whose_step_landed` at :4550.
- **Extend via:** New main-thread work that moves pages goes after this call and before the next submit. The tempting duplicate is a second wait on `commit_done`, or a new event for one feature.
- **Breaks if:** Page-moving work runs on the plan thread or the GPU thread before admit, or a new GPU-thread exit path skips releasing the event, in which case the fence waits out its timeout and logs. This fence shares its event with the `MSTAR_LAUNCH_WAIT_MS` GIL throttle at :4733-4734 but is a separate wait with a separate purpose (docstring at :2225-2227).
- **See also:** `worker-scheduling.md` › Teardown ordering: admit fence, deferred removes, drains, 13. Stamped teardown ordering, `lifecycle.md` › Two-phase teardown barrier, `lifecycle.md` › Worker removal sequencing.

### 13. Stamped teardown ordering
- **Does:** Has the leader forward removals and drains to its followers, stamping each removal with the last seq it broadcast, and has followers tear down only at that point in the step stream and in the leader's order.
- **Where:** `RemoveRequest.after_tp_seq` at ipc_format.py:68 and `MessageSource` at :56. `Worker._remove_request` at worker.py:929 holds the follower guard (:941-958), the in-order deferral (:966-974) and the leader's stamped forward (:976-1001). `_removal_step_reached` at :4033, `_apply_removes_whose_step_landed` at :4041 and `_apply_pending_removes_safe_to_drop` at :4054 apply held removals, and `MicroScheduler.last_consumed_tp_seq` at micro_scheduler.py:333 is the follower's clock. Drains forward through `_drain_request` (:1026) and `_begin_drain` (:1045).
- **Keeps:** Pages return to the free list at the same point in the step sequence and in the same order on every rank, so the ranks keep handing out the same page ids. A follower does not ACK `READS_DONE` while a committed follow batch for the rid is still queued (worker.py:1095).
- **Used by:** The main loop at worker.py:4533, :4550 and :4788-4794, and `_handle_allocation_failure` at :2356.
- **Extend via:** A new leader-side release that followers must mirror is forwarded from rank 0 with `MessageSource.TP_RANK_0` and an `after_tp_seq` stamp, and the follower applies it through `_removes_awaiting_step`. Its tests are test/modular/test_tp_async_teardown_order.py. The tempting duplicates are a follower acting on the conductor's copy, or a separate seq counter for one kind of teardown. Invariant 11's teardown lesson is the background.
- **Breaks if:** A follower frees request state outside the stamp, for example on a local failure. `last_consumed_tp_seq` never advances on a leader, so the stamp check must stay follower-only (worker.py:947-949).
- **See also:** `worker-scheduling.md` › Teardown ordering: admit fence, deferred removes, drains, 12. Admit fence, `lifecycle.md` › Two-phase teardown barrier, `lifecycle.md` › Worker removal sequencing.

### 14. TP async scheduling protocol
- **Does:** With `MSTAR_TP_ASYNC_SCHED` on, has the leader speculate step N+1 during N and broadcast it at once as a head with `spec_from_seq` N, and send exactly one of a head or a `TPNoSpeculation` marker for every leader step, so followers rebuild N+1 during their own N.
- **Where:** The setting is parsed at worker.py:174 and :187, read at :579-581, and tested per node by `_tp_async_for` at :2463. Leader side: `_can_speculate` at :2433, the head at :4280, `_tp_lead_needs_marker` at :2498 and `_broadcast_tp_nospec` at :1776. Follower side: `_await_tp_follow_step` at :3128, `_try_follow_speculation` at :2925, `_register_tp_nospec` at :3083, `_register_tp_follow` at :3088, `_close_tp_follow_step` at :3108 and `_step_voids_head` at :3119. `TPNoSpeculation` is at ipc_format.py:203.
- **Keeps:** Each parallel step a follower awaits settles on exactly one leader decision before N is post-processed, and every post-N verdict that voids a head (admit error, failed rows) is derived identically on each rank, with no commit or cancel message (worker.py:2920-2923). Heads are same-node loop-backs only (:2933-2942). The thread and stage order is invariant 7.
- **Used by:** The main loop at worker.py:4593-4601 and the exception path at :4875-4876. The startup agreement check is in block 5.
- **Extend via:** A new reason for the leader to clear its speculation has to be computable by the follower in `_step_voids_head`, or the leader sends a marker instead of a head. Its tests are test/modular/test_tp_async_follow_sched.py, test_tp_async_follow_verdict.py, test_tp_async_lockstep_sim.py and test_tp_async_sched_agreement.py. The tempting duplicate is a commit or cancel message after N.
- **Breaks if:** The leader leaves a step with neither a head nor a marker, so the follower waits and warns every 2 s (:3178-3187), or the leader clears a head on a verdict the follower cannot see. A later seq with no decision is read as no-spec (:3161-3173), which hides a flag mismatch instead of failing. See also the first finding below.
- **See also:** `worker-scheduling.md` › TP lockstep follow, 6. ScheduleTPNode broadcast, 7. Follower FIFO and serial follow path, 8. Follower settle seam.

### 15. Collectives inside a compiled, captured step
- **Does:** Runs a collective inside a `torch.compile`d forward that a CUDA graph captures.
- **Where:** `allreduce_oneshot` and `allreduce_add_rmsnorm` at mstar/distributed/ar_fusion.py:85 and :115 are `torch.library.custom_op`s with a `register_fake`, "so dynamo and CUDA-graph capture see one opaque node" (module docstring). `CommGroup.all_gather` and `reduce_scatter` (communication.py:101, :180) call `dist.*_tensor` directly, which dynamo in torch 2.12.1 traces as functional collectives. Main's `CommGroup.all_to_all` (communication.py:211) calls the list `dist.all_to_all`, which dynamo can't trace, so the graph breaks there. #897 moves it to `all_to_all_single` behind `@torch.compiler.disable`.
- **Keeps:** A correct served result. In the #897 review (Cosmos3 SP2, H100, torch 2.12.1), the Ulysses all-to-all inside the compiled captured step gave all-black images both traced by dynamo and as a `torch.library.custom_op`, though the traced form was bit-exact in a standalone compile-and-capture test.
