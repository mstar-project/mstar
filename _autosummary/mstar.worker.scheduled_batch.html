mstar.worker.scheduled_batch#

A step’s rows as the scheduler hands them on, and the chunk arithmetic that goes with them. Below the scheduler and Lockstep, which both build batches.

loop_strides(rids)[source]#

RouteInput.loop_strides for rids, in that order: a row of a chunk-Loop walk moves its Loop by its chunk (chunk_loop_stride), and any other row its loop by one. None for a step with no row in a chunk Loop, which is every decode step.

Keyed on the walk having a Loop rather than on the row having a chunk range: a row with no range ran whole, which also ends the Loop – a walk with no token budget is never measured or cut.
