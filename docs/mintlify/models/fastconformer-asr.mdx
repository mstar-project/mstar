## `canary`

FastConformer encoder + Transformer decoder: speech recognition in 25 European languages and translation to or from English (`language`, English if unset, and `target_language` or `task="translate"`). Clips up to 40 s; longer uploads are windowed. No timestamps.

Checkpoint: `nvidia/canary-1b-v2`.

## `canary_qwen`

English speech recognition by an LLM: Canary's FastConformer encoder projected into Qwen3-1.7B (LoRA merged at load). Clips up to 40 s; longer uploads are windowed. The OpenAI `prompt` is ignored. No timestamps.

Checkpoint: `nvidia/canary-qwen-2.5b`.

## `parakeet`

FastConformer + token-and-duration transducer, 25 European languages, language detected from the speech. Clips up to 10 min; longer uploads are windowed. No timestamps.

Checkpoint: `nvidia/parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v3`.

## `nemotron_asr`

Cache-aware streaming FastConformer + RNN-T with a language prompt (`language`: a locale such as `en-US`, a bare code, or `auto`); served over whole clips, with the streaming model's chunked attention at its widest lookahead (`lookahead` in the YAML's `model_kwargs`). Clips up to 10 min; longer uploads are windowed. No timestamps.

Checkpoint: `nvidia/nemotron-3.5-asr-streaming-0.6b`.

## Deployment configs

The CLI maps the model keys to these deployment configs. See [Installation](/installation) for dependencies; model-specific requirements and limits follow on this page:

| CLI model key  | Config in the repository    |
|----------------|-----------------------------|
| `canary`       | `configs/canary.yaml`       |
| `canary_qwen`  | `configs/canary_qwen.yaml`  |
| `parakeet`     | `configs/parakeet.yaml`     |
| `nemotron_asr` | `configs/nemotron_asr.yaml` |

From the repository root, launch a deployment with:

``` bash
mstar serve canary
```

For client requests and response formats, see [Using a Server](/clients).

## Install audio dependencies

Install with `.[asr]`. Uploads that libsndfile can't read (M4A, WebM) are decoded by torchcodec, which needs FFmpeg.

## GPU placement

One GPU per deployment: both nodes share a rank, and there is no tensor parallelism.

## Encoder implementation

All four run the FastConformer encoder `nemotron_duplex` streams (`mstar/model/components/fastconformer.py`), over the whole clip. Canary, Parakeet and Nemotron ASR read its dimensions from the HF `encoder_config`; Canary-Qwen from NeMo's `perception.encoder` (`FastConformerConfig.from_nemo`), which refuses an encoder whose parts NeMo configures as partly causal.

## Language and task fields

Request fields, the same on `/v1/audio/transcriptions` and in `/generate`'s `model_kwargs`:

- `parakeet`: detects the language from the speech. `language` may name one of the 25 it knows (bg, hr, cs, da, nl, en, et, fi, fr, de, el, hu, it, lv, lt, mt, pl, pt, ro, sk, sl, es, sv, ru, uk) and is then ignored; any other is refused.
- `nemotron_asr`: `language` is its prompt: a locale from the checkpoint's `processor_config.json` (such as `en-US`), a bare code, or `auto` (the default).
- `canary`: `language` (the speech's, default `en`; one of Parakeet's 25), `target_language` (a translation to or from English; the OpenAI route also takes `task="translate"`) and `punctuation` (default on).
- `canary_qwen`: English only. `instruction` replaces the trained one, "Transcribe the following:"; `/generate`'s text is unused.

## Audio duration and timestamps

A request holds up to 610 s on Parakeet and Nemotron, and 40.96 s on Canary and Canary-Qwen. `/v1/audio/transcriptions` cuts longer uploads (up to 30 min) into windows at quiet moments and joins their text. None of the four emits timestamps.

## Transducer state and decoding

Parakeet and Nemotron hold no cache. Each clip's decoder state (the LSTM's, and the cursor) sits in a recurrent state slot from its first decode iteration until the request is torn down; the YAMLs size `resources.transducer_state.max_slots` (129) for `max_concurrent_requests` (128) and the sink slot, and startup refuses a cap the pool can't hold. A decode iteration is 64 greedy steps, and its tokens stream out as it ends. `compile_step: false` in their `model_kwargs` decodes with eager kernels instead of the compiled step.

## Canary cache capacity

Canary's and Canary-Qwen's YAMLs size the KV caches for `max_concurrent_requests` (64), and startup refuses a cap the caches can't hold.

## Canary-Qwen memory sizing

Canary-Qwen's default KV cache takes about 15 GB (64 requests at 2048 tokens); on a smaller GPU, lower `max_concurrent_requests` and `resources.kv_cache.max_num_pages` together.

## Validation and sampling limits

Request knobs must have their documented types (a string `language`, a boolean `punctuation`, a string `instruction`), or the request is a 400. Canary-Qwen refuses a `language` other than English on every route. Canary and Canary-Qwen decode greedily unless the request sets `temperature` above 0 (`top_p` and `top_k` then apply); neither checkpoint declares sampling defaults, so a `min_p` or `repetition_penalty` is refused. `max_output_tokens` may be at most 1014 on Canary (the position table less its 10-token prompt) and 1024 on Canary-Qwen; a larger value is refused rather than cut. Parakeet and Nemotron decode greedily and read only `language`: a sent `temperature` is logged as ignored.
